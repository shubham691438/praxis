## Decisions made

- **Maven over Gradle** — reproducibility, enterprise-standard, declarative. ADR 0002.
- **Java 21 LTS** — already installed, virtual threads are final. Migrating to 25 later becomes
  its own ADR and a "I upgraded a live system's runtime" story.
- **Modular monolith first, split in Phase 3** — so I learn *why* services get split, and earn
  the right to say "I extracted services when I had evidence I needed them."
- **AI content generated on demand via the Claude API** — infinite coverage, small codebase.
